High Pressure Expanded is the high-pressure end of the steam chain: a bigger boiler that raises steam into the high band, and an engine efficient enough to spend it well.

It was split out of Low Pressure Expanded on purpose. Most players stay low-tech, so high pressure is opt-in - if you never install this mod you still get a complete low-pressure steam plant, and the mods below it in the chain never depend on it. Existing worlds are migrated automatically: a Lancashire boiler or Cornish engine you already built keeps working after you add this mod.

Compatibility

  • Game version: built for Vintage Story 1.22. Legacy 1.20 and 1.21 builds are on the GitHub releases page.
  • Multiplayer: works in singleplayer and on dedicated servers.
  • Requires: Expanded Library (exlib), Ironworking Expanded (iwex), Low Pressure Expanded (lpex) and Steelmaking Expanded (smex).
  • Languages: English, Russian, Ukrainian.
  • Save safety: safe to add to an existing world. Removing it leaves the placed machines behind, so break them first if you uninstall.

What it adds

  • Lancashire boiler - the heavy twin-flue boiler. Up to 48 L/s of steam, chokes at 12 atm, a 1200 L vessel that needs 200 L before it will boil. Raised through right-click construction stages over a fire-brick firebox exactly like the Cornish boiler, and just as explosive when left over-pressured. Takes an iron-tier pickaxe to mine.
  • Cornish engine - the efficient high-pressure beam engine. Right-click it with a wrench to raise its steam control rods and ctrl+right-click to lower them; the three settings (Low / Normal / High) draw 8 / 16 / 32 L/s of steam for 0.2 / 0.4 / 0.8 units of power. Throttling up raises the pressure the engine needs to engage as well as what it drinks. Drives any of the sub-machines - MP generator, fluid pump, or the air blower from Steelmaking Expanded.

How it works

There is no separate high-pressure network. The pipe network is one live pressure pool, and "high pressure" is simply steam carried above the low tier's band. Each machine gates on its own inlet pressure rather than on a different medium, so an HP machine and an LP machine can sit on the same run - which is exactly why you have to think about it.

The two machines are built for each other, but not blindly: the Lancashire's 12 atm ceiling sits well above the Cornish engine's 8 atm breaking point, so put a pressure valve between them, with an overflow pipe, exactly as you would between a Cornish boiler and a Watt engine. Everything else you already know from the low-pressure mod - construction stages, fireboxes, priming with buckets, sub-machine placement, wrench repairs - applies here unchanged.

Commands and config

  • /exmod config hpex - view or change any gameplay value live, applied immediately with no world reload (admin).
  • /exmod recipes hpex <normal|cheap> - switch the crafting and construction cost level; applies on the next world reload (admin).

Both live as an hpex section inside the shared ex_values.json / ex_recipes.json under VintagestoryData/ModConfig/.

FAQ

  • My Cornish engine will not start. Its engage pressure rises with the throttle - Low engages around 5 atm, High wants 7. Either feed it more pressure or throttle it down with a wrench.
  • My Cornish engine broke. It ran above 8 atm for too long. Gate the line with a pressure valve. Right-click it with a wrench to see the repair bill, then again with the materials in your hotbar.
  • My Lancashire boiler exploded. It kept firing with nowhere to send its steam. Open the lid to vent, or gate the steam line.
  • I upgraded and my machines vanished. They should not - the block codes are migrated on world load, including from worlds that predate the mod split. If something did slip through, replace it in creative and please report it.
