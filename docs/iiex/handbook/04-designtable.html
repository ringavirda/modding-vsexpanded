The Design Table is where plans are drawn. A diagram is a reusable crafting plan: it goes in the grid alongside the raw material, tells the recipe which shape to make, and - because it is a tool, not an ingredient - it is not consumed. Draw a plan once and build from it forever.

Why bother. Without diagrams, every shape of every part needs its own grid pattern, and a pipe network's four shapes across three material tiers is twelve patterns to memorise. With them there is one pattern per part - plan plus material - and the plan picks the shape. The same straight-canal diagram cuts a straight canal out of any rock you feed it.

Blocks required:
- 1 x Design Table

Using a diagram. Put it in the crafting grid with the material the recipe calls for - for a molten canal, cobblestone. The output takes the shape the diagram names and the material you supplied, and the diagram comes back out unharmed.

This is a young system. The molten canal shapes are the first parts to use it, and more will move over. Existing hand-patterned recipes keep working alongside it, so nothing you have already learned stops working.
