Ironworking Expanded is the iron tier: everything you need to smelt iron in bulk before you own a single boiler. It is deliberately self-sufficient - the whole chain runs off an axle and a waterwheel, and nothing in it needs steam.

The loop. Crushed iron ore, crushed coke and lime flux go into an Ore Mixer, which blends them into burden - the prepared charge. Burden is stockpiled in an Ore Bunker and dropped into the Cold Blast Furnace, which is blown with cold air from a Twin-Tub Blower and renders the charge down to molten pig iron and slag. The pig runs out of a tap into a molten canal, which plumbs it to wherever you want to cast it.

Pig iron is not a finished metal. It is hard, brittle and useless for tools - it is the shared intermediate everything downstream is made from. Remelt it in a Cupola Furnace with scrap and you get cast iron, which casts into machine parts and substitutes for vanilla iron in build recipes. Work it down instead and you get wrought iron, which is vanilla iron. Steel comes later, and needs the steam tier.

What the iron tier leaves behind. Bolted pipe - the first of three pipe tiers - and the molten-canal network, both of which every later tier builds on. Cast iron tools and parts. And slag, which is not waste: it lays as paths, slabs and stairs.

Where to start. Build the ore mixer first - nothing else works without burden. Then the furnace, then a blower on an axle. The canal can wait: pig solidifies at the tap and can be chiselled out by hand until you have somewhere better to send it.
