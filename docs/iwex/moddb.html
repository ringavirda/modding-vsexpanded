The iron tier for Vintage Story: a full ironmaking chain that runs on nothing but an axle and a waterwheel. Blend ore, coke and flux into a prepared burden, smelt it in a cold blast furnace, and plumb the molten pig iron out through cast canals to wherever you want to cast it. No steam required - and the pipe and molten-canal networks it lays down are what every later tier is built on.

Compatibility

  • Game version: built for Vintage Story 1.22. Legacy 1.20 and 1.21 builds are on the GitHub releases page.
  • Multiplayer: works in singleplayer and on dedicated servers.
  • Requires: Expanded Library (exlib). Install it before updating.
  • Languages: English, Russian, Ukrainian.
  • Save safety: safe to add to an existing world. Removing it leaves the placed machine blocks behind, so break them first if you uninstall.

Quick start

The iron line, in order. Each step has a full handbook article ("Ironworking:") in game.

  1. Build an Ore Mixer and drive it from an axle. Feed it crushed iron ore, crushed coke and lime; it blends them into burden, which is the only thing the furnace eats.
  2. Build the Cold Blast Furnace: place the core block, hold Ctrl + Shift + right-click for the build outline, and fill in every highlighted block until it reports complete.
  3. Put a Tall Hopper over the shaft and an Ore Bunker beside it, so the furnace charges itself.
  4. Run plated pipe from a Twin-Tub Blower to the two tuyeres. Seal the run - an open pipe end vents to atmosphere and the line will never hold pressure.
  5. Ignite the charge. The furnace climbs on its own and enters Melting once it is clear of iron's melting point; a coke-rich burden gets there on cold blast, a lean one will not.
  6. Open the lower tap. Pig iron solidifies at the tap and chisels free, or runs into a molten canal and on to a mold pedestal.

What it adds

  • Cold blast furnace - an open-topped multiblock that smelts ore burden to molten pig iron, with no fixed maximum temperature: what it reaches is a live balance of fuel, blast and heat loss, and block-info shows both sides of it.
  • Cupola furnace - the smaller re-melter: pig plus scrap into castable cast iron.
  • Ore mixer and ore bunker - burden preparation and buffering, with the mix stamped onto the charge so the furnace can read it.
  • Molten canals - per-cell liquid metal transport, with taps, mold pedestals and barrels. Cuttable from cobblestone, running brick or fire brick.
  • Plated pipe - the first of three pipe tiers, and the twin-tub blower that fills it.
  • Cast iron - a full tool and part family, and a substitute for vanilla iron in build recipes.
  • Slag - a byproduct with a use: paths, slabs and stairs.
  • Diagrams and the design table - reusable crafting plans that pick a part's shape, so one grid pattern covers a whole family.
