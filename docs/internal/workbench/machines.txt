Filler layouts for machines

Legend:
    "." - empty space
    "O" - principal block in north orientation
    "M" - full block filler with mp energy connection on north and south sides
    "m" - full block filler with mp energy connection on east and west sides
    "P" - full block filler with pipe connection
    "I" - full block filler with main machine interactions
    "i" - full block filler with additional machine interactions
    "#" - full block filler
    "-" - vertical north slab filler
    "_" - horizontal bottom slab filler


Mp bending machine
Purpose: 
Consumes plates, boilerplates, rods, beams and other similar items and
produces bend variants after several passes.
Mode of operation: 
Rmb on input, resulting item is thrown from output. Rollers don't wear down.
Note: 
"I" here is loading plates, rods or beams, that need to be bended and that
get thrown from the other side. Which cell of two is currently intractable is
based on input shaft rotation direction (can only be one at a time). 
"i" is the cell that has interaction about setting the height (gap) of the
movable roller. Player pushes it down to achieve bigger bending curve.
Layout (XZ layers, north is up):
    L1:
        # M #
        I O I
        - - -
    L2:
        _ # _
        . i .
        . # .
    L3:
        . _ .
        . # .
        . _ .


Mp cutter (mp shears)
Purpose:
Consumes rolled stock and converts it into cut stock or final items. Cuts one
item from stock at a time.
Mode of operation: 
Put item in window interface, pull resulting item after machine finishes
operating. Shears wear down and need replacing, shear material affects how long
they serve.
Note: 
"O" here also has north-south mp connection. 
"I" here is where player gets window interface, puts items that need to be cut 
into parts and where cut items get thrown out. Also here player replaces worn
out shear blades or places new ones.
Layout (XY slice, north is forward):
    S1:
        _ _ _
        I O #


Mp drilling press
Purpose:
Consumes items and shapes them by drilling holes, canals or removing small
parts. Required for items with complex shapes.
Mode of operation: 
Put item in window interface, pull resulting item after machine finishes
operating. Drill bit wears down and need replacing, material affects how it 
serves.
Note:
"I" here is not a full block, but north vertical slab. It contains window
interactions for putting items, removing finished ones, and replacing drill bit.
Layout (YZ slice, north is right):
    S1:
    # # #
    I # #
    I O M


Mp horizontal boring machine:
Purpose:
Special machine for drilling massive holes into big items or for shaping
cylinders to precision.
Mode of operation: 
Put item in window interface, pull resulting item after machine finishes
operating. Cutter of the bore wears down and need replacing, material affects 
how long it serves.
Note:
"I" here is main window interface where player places worked items, removes
finished ones and replaces machines's cutter.
"i" is where player needs to hold rmb to move bed and actually operate the
machine. Without player holding rmb on this cutter rotates, but nothing happens.
It is also a vertical north slab filler.

Layout (XZ layers, north is up)
    L1:
        m O #
        . i .
    L2:
        # I #
        . . .

Mp rolling mill
Purpose:
Rolls stock items using flat, grooved or other rollers into shapes. Resulting
items often elongated and need cutting with mp cutter into final items.
Mode of operation:
Consumes stock items and produces flattened or grooved or other variants 
after several passes. Accepts items from one side, and throws out resulting
items from another.
Note:
"I*" here is where player inputs stock items. There are two of these, and which
one is active depends on input shaft rotation direction. The other one becomes
the cell where items get thrown out.
"I1" is south vertical slab filler.
"I2" is north vertical slab filler.
"i1" are fillers that hold interactions of razing and lowering of rollers if
current rollers are flat wide. (the same mechanic as for bending machine. this
way we can collapse all flat wide rollers into one item.) 
"i1" is where player interacts to replace rollers. If those are flat or grooved,
then those are locked in place. if those are flat wide, then top one can be
moved.
"-" are vertical north slab fillers.
"_" are vertical south slab fillers.
Layout (XZ layers, north is up):
    L1:
        _ I1 _
        m O m
        - I2 -
    L2:
        _ . _
        i1 i2 i1
        - . -

Pipe steam hammer
Purpose:
Shingling of bars and slabs, die stamping of general shapes.
Mode of operation:
Connect to pipe network with enough pressure steam.
If flat die is inserted, then put puddled ball, pig,
blister steel bar or other appropriate item, hold rmb to pull lever, and steam
hammer produces item according to recipe, after which resulting item is thrown
on the ground.
If stamp die is inserted, then blanks, plates or other appropriate items can be 
placed on the anvil, rmb to pull leaver and stamp them into shapes. Also recipe
based.
Recipes declare how many hits hammer should do.
Note:
"P" here has pipe connection on west side.
"O" has main interactions for placing items.
"i" is where player holds rmb to pull control leaver.
"|" is east vertical slab filler.
"-" is west vertical slab filler.
Layout (XY slice, north is front):
    S1:
        . # .
        . P .
        | # -
        i O #

Note:

"P" is output air pipe has pipe connection on north side.
"_" is down slab filler.

    S1:
        m # _
        O # P


Pipe mp fluid pump

Supply boilers and other water machines with water with mp power.
Mode of operation:
Connect to vanilla mp or mpenergy network.
Note:
"O" here has vanilla mp or mpenergy connection on west side.
"P" is full block fluid output cell, that has pipe connection on south face.
"p" is full block fluid input cell, that has pipe connection on bottom face.

    S1:
        . . P
        O # p

Pipe steam cornish boiler
Purpose: raise steam with enough pressure to drive basic Watt engine.
Mode of operation: Supply water with pipe through water input, supply and light
coal through main hatch, pipe output steam through output pipe, pipe output
exhaust through exhaust output pipe. Make sure boiler doesn't explode.
Note:
"_" here is horizontal bottom slab filler.
"I" is main interaction cell, that allows to open main hatch, input coal, fire
coal, close main hatch.
"E" is full filler with pipe connection at east, exhaust output.
"W" is full filler with pipe connection at south, water input. It is also principal.
"S" is full filler with pipe connection at up, steam output.
"M" is horizontal bottom slab filler, with man hatch interactions, that allow to
manually fill boiler with buckets or perform emergency venting of steam.
