1. Cupola: Ctrl+Shift+RMB a tap for the outline; "Structure is incomplete!" clears on the last brick.
2. Cupola: coke then pigs via the hopper (Ctrl+RMB for stacks); unplug the lower tap, torch it -> Firing; HUD reaches 1200 -> Melting.

5. Cell: ram greensand, imprint, feed from a canal on the facing side; watch "Casting: n/cap" and the glow.
6. Cell shake-out after hardening: part or scrap? Code predicts scrap every time.
7. Long cell: body runs away from you; cell-size pattern refused; count billets from a full pour (code predicts 1).
8. Bed: click sand to carve, pour, collect; check the "pig(s) ready" count and chunk/bit remainders.
9. Rack: lay a slab (whole rack) then billets; confirm items are drawn on the rails, any cell of a run gives it back, and whether a torch attaches to a filler cell.
10. Workbench: hold Craft on a vanilla 3x3 recipe - ~3 crafts/s into the output row, stopping on release.
