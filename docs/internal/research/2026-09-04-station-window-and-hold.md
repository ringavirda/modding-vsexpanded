Packet conventions to keep: `<1000` slot moves; `1000/1001` open/close (private in the station, equal to vanilla `EnumBlockEntityPacketId`); `1002 = FirstMachinePacketId` = the station's primary action (Draw/Craft today) - the boring-machine page already rules "a second station must use the same two and pick its own >= 1002" (`boring-machine.md:206`); reserve `1003` for picker selection if it must persist before the action. The hold itself needs no packet: block interaction is engine-synced, and the server-side `Step` is what stamps the holder. Open with the owner: whether the shear adopts the window (machining-line.md:379-381).
