## 2026-09-07 - exlib module system: identity, host, phases, contributors

Landed `2026-09-06-exlib-module-system.md`. `[assembly: ExModule("<id>")]` gives an assembly its own
identity (`Host`, the mod whose lifecycle drives it; `Mod`, the Vintage Story mod that ships it, so a
world's disabled mods do not drive a module left over from a previous one; `Requires`, ordered by
Kahn's algorithm with a missing requirement, a cycle or a duplicate id each excluded and logged by
name; `PatchHarmony`). `ExModules` discovers every module in the process once per assembly-count
change and orders each host's enabled set; `ExModuleHost` is the per-driver-instance runtime that
instantiates entry points and runs a module's registries (config, entities, commands, preferences,
Harmony) and phases (`StartPre`, `Start`, `AssetsLoaded`, `AssetsFinalize`,
`StartServerSide`/`StartClientSide`, `Dispose`) the same way `ExModSystem` runs them for a main
assembly. `IExDefinitionContributor` is discovered alongside a module's classes and run by
`ExDefinitionModSystem` right before injection at `AssetsLoaded` 0.04, regardless of host or module
order - Industry's metal-family emission moved there from its own `AssetsLoaded`. `samples/HelloModule`
proves the third-party shape (its own mod, its own domain, depending on exlib, a block behaviour
resolved cross-assembly through the module's `ExDomain`). Two engine findings recorded along the
way: a Code mod's dll needs at least one `ModSystem` to load at all - the loader refuses one with
none, `declared as code mod, but there are no .dll files that contain at least one ModSystem or has
a ModInfo attribute` - so a module shipped as its own mod carries an empty placeholder one; and
`AssetCatalogueLoader.GetMany` deserializes one file to one object, so a catalogue of several
entries needs one file per entry, not one file with several. An architect review after the first
four tasks returned three Major and eleven Minor findings, applied in one pass (enabled-mod
filtering was the significant one; the rest were doc/test-hygiene). Wiki: a new Modules page, plus
updates to Registries, Lifecycle, Code-First-Definitions, Getting-Started, Home, Supported-API and
the sidebar.

## 2026-09-05 - Casting bed drawn over its fillers

The bed's shape carried `AngleFromSide + 180` as well as its `StructureAngle`, so it drew its rows on the
side opposite the fillers that carve them, at every facing (north: draws z over [-0.5, 3.5] but reserves
[-3.5, 0.5]; south: [-3.5, 0.5] against [-0.5, 3.5]; east: draws x over [-3.5, 0.5] against [-0.5, 3.5];
west: draws x over [-0.5, 3.5] against [-3.5, 0.5]). The shape now spins by the side angle alone
(`ShapeSpunPerOrientation`, no offset) and `StructureAngle` keeps the +180; the blocktype golden was
re-blessed (`rotateYByType` only).

The rule: pair the shape's spin with `StructureAngle` only where the filler offsets are authored in the
shape's frame. Where the art grows one way and the footprint the other, the half turn goes on
`StructureAngle` alone, and then everything that follows the mesh turns by `Block.Shape.rotateY` while
everything that follows the footprint turns by `StructureAngle`. On the bed the mesh side is the per-cell
molten surfaces, which were spun by `StructureAngle` and are now spun by the shape's own angle.

The same half turn crosses the flanks over: a three-wide footprint turned 180 puts its west cells on the
east of the drawn bed, so `SandBedLayout` draws a west slot with `Mold{row}E` and an east slot with
`Mold{row}W`. Paired the other way the carve appeared across the spine from the cell that was clicked, at
all four facings. The whole-box comparison cannot see that, because the bed's footprint is symmetric
across the spine.

Guards: `CastingBedFootprintGuards` - `MegablockFrames.Misfit` null at all four sides, and every slot's
drawn element measured into the cell that carves it, in both the states that slot can hold, at all four
sides. Red before the fix with the extents above and with `A 'north' bed draws BedSlot { Row = 2, Side =
West } as Sand with 'Mold2WFull', centred (0.94, 1) blocks from the principal, but carves that slot at
cell (1, -1)`.

Walkthrough item A4 (runners and moulds read swapped east-west) is covered: the surface cuboid and the
HUD's mold count both tested `cellPos.X == Pos.X`, which picks out the spine only on a north- or
south-facing bed. Both now read the cell's slot, so a bed laid east or west gives its runners the channel
surface and counts its flanks.

## 2026-09-05 - Four walkthrough blockers fixed

Defect 1 (the helve route failed): the shingling work item was declared with vanilla's `ItemWorkItem`,
whose helve check answers NotWorkable to any recipe named neither plate nor blister steel. The item is now
its own registered `ItemShingleWorkItem` (code `shingleworkitem`, the metal as a variant), which answers
FullyWorkable; `IiexCollectibleClassTests` pins that every class string an iiex def names is registered,
and `PuddlingYieldTests` covers the item. Golden moved to `itemtypes/shingleworkitem.json`.

Defect 2 (casts reading as misruns): the misrun check ran at shake-out against the cell's live
temperature, by which time the metal had hardened far below any pour minimum, so every full cast was
scrapped. `BlockEntitySandCastingCell` now records the metal's temperature in the tick the cavity fills
(saved as `cc_filltemp`) and `CastingCellLogic.IsMisrun(cavityFull, pourTemp, minPourTemp)` judges that
value; a cell saved before the value existed shakes out clean. Tests: `CastingCellLogicTests`,
`CastingCellTests`.

Defect 3 (the long cell could not pour, and both cells drew their spout on the wrong face): the long
cell's shape carried the same +180 offset as its `StructureAngle`, so the body was drawn back onto the
footprint's own cell instead of opposite it - `The_drawn_mesh_lands_inside_the_reserved_footprint(side:
"north")` failed with `draws z over [-0.5, 1.5] but reserves [-1.5, 0.5] ... The shape spins by 180deg and
the footprint by 180deg`. Dropped the shape's offset so only `StructureAngle` carries the half turn.
Separately, `LaunderFace` read the block's raw facing rather than the face the launder spout is drawn on,
so the pull always looked at the filler cell (long cell) or the wrong side (both cells); `LaunderFace` now
turns the facing around. Guarded at every side, both cells, by `CastingCellFootprintGuards`, and pinned by
`The_long_cell_pulls_metal_fed_at_its_launder_face`. The long cell's blocktype golden was re-blessed
(`rotateYByType` only). The casting bed (`BlockSandCastingBed`) shows the identical fault at all four
sides (north: draws z over [-0.5, 3.5] but reserves [-3.5, 0.5]; south: draws z over [-3.5, 0.5] but
reserves [-0.5, 3.5]; east: draws x over [-3.5, 0.5] but reserves [-0.5, 3.5]; west: draws x over [-0.5,
3.5] but reserves [-3.5, 0.5]) - left unfixed, out of scope.

Defect 4 (a multi-lane pour yielded one piece): `PatternItemDefinitions.Mold` wrote its output stack with
no quantity, so the harvest's `Max(1, spec.Output.Quantity)` always fell back to one regardless of how
many lanes a pattern declared. `Mold` now carries an `outputQuantity` (3 for `castbillets`, 2 for
`castblooms`, 1 elsewhere) and the pattern golden was re-blessed. Tests:
`A_full_billet_pour_harvests_all_three_lanes`, `A_multi_lane_pattern_declares_a_lane_per_output`.
