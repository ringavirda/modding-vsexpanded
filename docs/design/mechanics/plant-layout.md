# Plant layout - and what a bank of machines does not buy

**Status** ruled 2026-08-05  **Mods** all
**Since** 2026-08-05

The shared rule that closes the absence flagged independently by [cupola.md](../machines/cupola.md)
(nothing rewards a bank of cupolas over one) and [ladle.md](../machines/ladle.md) (nothing rewards a
second ladle).

**Owns** - the facts this page is canonical for:

* whether a bank of machines is mechanically better than the sum of its units (it is not)
* what layout actually buys the player, and why that is not a bonus

---

## 1. The rule

A bank is throughput only. N machines do N times the work and nothing more.

Machines do not become more efficient for standing next to each other. There is no shared blast
manifold that makes two cupolas cheaper than two cupolas, no residual-heat ignition that makes the second
coke oven cost less than the first, no staggered-cadence bonus. A second machine is a second machine.

This closes, in one place, four separately-flagged notes: the cupola bank, the second ladle, the coke oven's
"light off the previous charge" idea, and the crucible room.

## 2. But layout is still a real decision - it is just not a number

What good layout buys is less hassle and smoother operation, not a multiplier. Where the rolling mill
sits relative to the reheat furnace, how far the pig travels from the casting bed to the cupola, whether
the shear sits at the end of the mill line or across the hall - all of that decides how much walking,
hauling and waiting the player does. None of it is a stat. It is emergent, and it belongs to the player.

| | |
|---|---|
| **the reward** | shorter hauls, fewer interruptions, a line that flows in one direction |
| **the mechanism** | none. It falls out of distance, capacity and cadence, which already exist |
| **who solves it** | the player. How to organise the works is the question the mod is asking |

The mod builds machines with honest properties and leaves the factory to the player; a layout bonus would
replace that question with a lookup table.

## 3. What this means for the pages that promised otherwise

Strike the promise, keep the flavour. "Build several" stays true as a description - one machine is not
enough throughput - but it must stop being written as though the game rewards the grouping. Any page that
implies a bank is more than the sum of its parts should say plainly that it is not.

Retired: the coke oven's "lit off the residual heat of the previous charge, which is a nice reason to
build a bank and keep it running". That oven's ignition is a question about that oven, with no bank
argument attached.

Not affected: shared infrastructure the player builds - one line shaft driving six stands, one gas main
feeding four burners, one canal serving two beds. That is the plumbing being a network, which the mod
already models and charges for. The distinction: shared infrastructure is built and paid for; a bank bonus
would be free for standing machines together.
